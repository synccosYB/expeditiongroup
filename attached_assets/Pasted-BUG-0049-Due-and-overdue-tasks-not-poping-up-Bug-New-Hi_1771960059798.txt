Description

Due and overdue tasks not poping up

AI Analysis

**Analysis:**

### 1. **Affected Part of the App:**
- The **Dashboard** component, specifically the "Due & Overdue Tasks" widget.

### 2. **UI State & Explanation:**
- The "Due & Overdue Tasks" widget displays:  
  `"No tasks are due or overdue right now."`
- However, the summary statistics at the top show:
  - **Pending Tasks:** "14 Due/Overdue" (top summary card)
- This mismatch suggests that there *are* due or overdue tasks, but they're not appearing in the dashboard widget.

### 3. **Root Cause Analysis:**
- **Data Sync Issue:** The count at the top (14) and the widget (0) are out of sync.
- **Potential Causes:**
  - **Frontend Filtering:** The widget may be receiving the data but incorrectly filtering/processing it, causing it to display zero results.
  - **API Response/Query:** The API request for the widget may not be returning the correct tasks, possibly because of the wrong date filter, incorrect status filter, or a background fetch error.
  - **State Management:** The task count and widget may be using different sources or queries for their data (e.g., top cards fetch all tasks, widget fetches a filtered subset).

### 4. **Recommended Fix or Next Steps:**
- **Check API Queries:** Ensure the queries powering the summary card and widget are consistent about what counts as "due/overdue."
- **Verify Filters:** Review frontend logic in the "Due & Overdue Tasks" widget to ensure it's accurately filtering for both "due" and "overdue" tasks.
- **Debug Data Flow:** Use browser dev tools to watch network requests and compare responses for summary cards vs. widget.
- **Console Errors:** No visible errors in the screenshot; check the browser console for silent fetch errors during the widget's render.
