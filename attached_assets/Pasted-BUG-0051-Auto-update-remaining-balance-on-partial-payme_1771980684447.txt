BUG-0051
Auto-update remaining balance on partial payments

Status

Description

When collecting payment under Undeposited Funds > Receive Payment and selecting an invoice, if a partial payment is entered, the system should automatically display only the remaining balance due each time an additional partial payment is made.

AI Analysis

**UI State Description:**
- The screenshot shows the "Undeposited Funds" section under "BOOKKEEPING."
- "Payments Pending Deposit" are listed with details (payment type, client, linked invoice, amount).
- There’s no visible balance breakdown per invoice or any dynamic display for remaining invoice balances after partial payments.
- There are no error messages or visual indications of processing errors in the UI.

**Bug Analysis:**
1. **Affected area:**  
   The issue is with the *Receive Payment* flow (specifically when handling partial payments against invoices).

2. **Root cause:**  
   The system does not refresh or dynamically update the "remaining balance due" for an invoice when a partial payment is entered and processed. Once a partial payment is made, users expect the UI to reflect the new balance due on the invoice. From the screenshot, it's not clear if there is a remaining balance field directly visible during payment collection, but the lack of automation is the core issue described.

   Likely causes:
   - The client-side logic/form may not recalculate the outstanding balance after posting a partial payment.
   - The UI may not be fetching or rendering the updated invoice balance after each transaction.
   - The backend may update the invoice balance correctly, but the change is not pushed or retrieved for real-time display.

3. **Recommended fix / next steps:**
   - **Frontend:** Update the payment form workflow so when a payment is submitted (especially partial), the UI automatically fetches and displays the new remaining balance without requiring a manual refresh. This can be achieved with:
     - A call to fetch the invoice status after payment submission,
     - Or, real-time computation by the client using the known invoice total and cumulative payments.
   - **Backend:** Verify that the API responding with invoice/payment details provides the current remaining balance.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** After any partial payment, the "remaining balance due" field (or equivalent) visibly and immediately shows the updated amount for the invoice in the payment collection UI.
