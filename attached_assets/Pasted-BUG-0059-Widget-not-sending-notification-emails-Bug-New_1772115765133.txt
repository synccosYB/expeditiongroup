Description

Not receiving email notifications from the widget when items are opened, resolved, or updated. Needs to be checked to ensure notification settings and email integration are functioning properly.

AI Analysis

1) What part of the app is affected:
The notification and email integration component of the widget is affected. Specifically, the logic responsible for sending email notifications when items are opened, resolved, or updated.

2) Root cause analysis:
Based on the report, the most likely causes include:
- Notification settings not configured properly (user/service-level preferences might prevent emails from being sent).
- Email integration misconfigured (SMTP settings, API keys, or connection issues).
- Backend logic failure (notification triggers may not be firing when item state changes occur).
- Email queue or service may be down (emails could be stuck in queue or failed to send).
Because no errors or logs are provided, further investigation is required:
- Check notification settings for the affected widget and user(s).
- Review email integration setup and logs for failures.
- Confirm that item status changes occur as expected and trigger notification events.

3) Recommended fix or next steps:
- Verify widget notification settings are enabled for email notifications.
- Confirm email integration credentials/configuration is valid.
- Review backend logs to ensure notification triggers are firing and not failing silently.
- Test item state changes (open, resolve, update) and monitor email delivery.
- If needed, add logging or error handling in notification/email service for better visibility.
