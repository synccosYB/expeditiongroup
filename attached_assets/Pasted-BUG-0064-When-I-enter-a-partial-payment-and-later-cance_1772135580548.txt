Task Description:
When I enter a partial payment and later cancel that payment in the register, the payment gets deleted, but the invoice balance does not update accordingly.

In this case:

Total Mega bill: $4,558.12

Payments made: $3,521.14 + $87.00

Remaining balance showed: $949.98

After canceling the $87.00 payment, the remaining balance should update to $1,036.98, but it is still showing $949.98.

Please review and correct the invoice balance so it reflects the accurate remaining amount.

AI Analysis

**UI State & Visible Errors:**
- The "Bills" screen lists several vendors and their bill statuses.
- For "MegaPay USA Inc." (the lower entry, #193530), the total bill is $4,558.12 and it displays **Balance: $949.98**.
- The payment of $87.00 made earlier is listed as a separate bill above.
- No UI error notifications are visible; the issue relates to an incorrect balance calculation.

**Analysis:**

1. **What part of the app is affected:**
   - The "Bills" section, specifically the calculation and display of the remaining balance on partially paid invoices after a payment cancellation.

2. **Root cause analysis:**
   - When a partial payment is cancelled, the payment record is deleted as expected. However, the invoice balance does **not** update, continuing to reflect the original reduced amount (as if the payment was still active).
   - Evidence: After cancelling the $87.00 payment, the balance should recalculate to $1,036.98 but stays at $949.98 (i.e., $87 lower than it should).
   - This suggests the front-end is not re-calculating or re-fetching the invoice balance based on current payment data, or the backend is failing to update the balance field.
   - Likely causes:
     - The payment removal event does not properly trigger a recalculation/update of the invoice balance.
     - There might be a caching issue or missing event/listener.
     - The bills section could be using a stale version of payment data, not reflecting the latest payment status.
     - Alternatively, the backend is not properly updating the invoice balance field upon payment deletion, so the front end continues to display outdated information.

3. **Recommended fix or next steps:**
   - **Backend:** Ensure that when a payment is cancelled/deleted, the associated invoice's balance is recalculated in the database. Removing a payment should update the sum of all active payments and adjust the invoice balance.
   - **Frontend:** After payment cancellation, re-fetch or refresh the bill data to reflect the current balance.
   - **Dev QA:** Test payment creation and deletion flows to verify that the invoice balance is consistent in all views.
   - **Optional:** Add a confirmation/refresh mechanism to force data sync whenever payment records change.
