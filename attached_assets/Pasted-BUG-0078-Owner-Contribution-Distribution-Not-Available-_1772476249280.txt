Description

When going to My Account > Register and adding a transaction, the Owner Contribution and Owner Distribution accounts do not appear in the account dropdown list.

As a result, I am unable to properly record when I personally deposit money into the business (Owner Contribution) or withdraw money from the business (Owner Distribution). The system is not displaying these chart of account options when entering a new transaction.

AI Analysis

### Analysis of the Bug Report:

1. **Affected Area:**
   - The issue lies in the **My Account > Register > Add Transaction** section of the application.
   - Specifically, the dropdown for selecting accounts when adding a new transaction does not include "Owner Contribution" or "Owner Distribution" accounts from the chart of accounts.

2. **Root Cause Analysis:**
   - From the provided details and screenshot:
     - The Owner Contribution and Owner Distribution accounts are likely not being fetched or filtered correctly when populating the dropdown list in the transaction entry interface.
     - This could happen because:
       - These specific account types are excluded by mistake, either due to a misconfigured query or a frontend filter in the dropdown population logic.
       - There may be a categorization issue where Owner Contribution/Distribution accounts are set up incorrectly in the underlying chart of accounts, making them unavailable for filtering or fetching.
       - A permissions issue may exist where these accounts are not accessible to the user role associated with the logged-in user.

3. **Recommended Fix or Next Steps:**
   - **Backend Validation:**
     - Verify that "Owner Contribution" and "Owner Distribution" accounts exist and are correctly categorized in the chart of accounts database table.
     - Check the API endpoint or query responsible for fetching the accounts to ensure that these types of accounts are included.
   - **Frontend Validation:**
     - Ensure the dropdown logic does not apply over-restrictive filters that exclude these account types.
   - **Role/Permission Check:**
     - Confirm that the logged-in user has appropriate permissions to access all account types, including Owner Contribution and Owner Distribution.
   - **User Communication:**
     - If this behavior is intentional and these accounts are not supposed to appear in this view, provide tooltips or user guidance to prevent confusion.
