Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open the Phone application on your device.
2. Access the widget within the Phone application.
3. Submit a "Back of Future" request using the widget.
4. Attempt to navigate back or access other pages after the request is submitted.
5. Try to capture or take action on the page using the phone application.

**Expected Behavior:**
- After submitting the "Back of Future" request, the widget should allow navigation to other pages or sections within the Phone application.
- The application should allow capturing or interacting with the page as expected.

**Actual Behavior:**
- After submitting the "Back of Future" request, the app remains stuck on the same page and does not allow navigation to other sections.
- The page cannot be captured or interacted with through the phone application.

**Additional Context:**
- Occurs specifically after submitting the "Back of Future" request via the widget.
- No error messages appear, but the app stays locked on the same screen and becomes partially unresponsive.

AI Analysis

### Bug Analysis:

1. **Part of the App Affected**:
   The bug affects navigation and interaction within the Phone application's widget functionality. This specifically occurs after submitting a "Back of Future" request.

2. **Root Cause Analysis**:
   Given the absence of console errors or detailed logs, the issue seems to be tied to a state-lock or improper state transition after the "Back of Future" request submission. Possible causes include:
   - The widget enters a "waiting state" after submission but fails to execute or complete the expected transition back to a neutral/ready state.
   - Navigation handlers or state resetting logic does not fire after the request, leaving the app unresponsive or stuck.
   - Certain post-submission processes (e.g., callback execution) might be timing out or failing silently.

   Since the user also cannot interact with or capture the page afterward, this could indicate issues with input handling being temporarily blocked, such as a non-dismissable modal screen or an overlay improperly lingering.

3. **Recommended Fix / Next Steps**:
   - Investigate the state management and navigation handlers post-submission of the "Back of Future" request. Validate whether the app transitions correctly and returns to a stable, interactive state.
   - Check the widget's "busy" or "loading" indicators. Confirm whether these are cleared and dismissed after submission.
   - Review associated logs or set up debugging to catch potential errors in the request's callback handling or UI update logic.
   - Test the widget under different configurations to see if the issue consistently reproduces and verify whether this issue is tied to timeout-related conditions or specific use cases.
