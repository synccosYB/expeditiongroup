Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Generate an invoice in the system.
2. Attempt to print the invoice.
3. Observe the behavior related to saving the invoice with its unique number.

**Expected Behavior:**
When an invoice is printed, the system should automatically save the invoice with its unique invoice number, and the saved file should be readily accessible in the designated folder.

**Actual Behavior:**
The system does not automatically save the invoice with its unique invoice number after printing. Instead, users are required to manually enter the invoice number in the folder for saving.

**Additional Context:**
This functionality used to work but has recently stopped. The manual step to input the invoice number for saving adds unnecessary effort and increases the risk of errors.

AI Analysis

### Analysis

1. **App Section Affected**:
   - Invoices Module, particularly during the printing and saving process.

2. **Root Cause Analysis**:
   - Based on the behavior described, the issue seems to stem from the automation logic that handles post-printing invoice saving. A few potential causes could include:
     - A regression in the backend or frontend triggered by recent updates that removed or bypassed the automatic saving functionality.
     - Misconfigured file-saving path or naming convention logic, potentially leading to unexpected errors.
     - A missing callback function or event handler for the "Print" button that used to invoke the saving mechanism.

   - The screenshot reveals the "Print" button is still present, and the invoice details (including the unique invoice number, e.g., `INV-2026-0076`, and associated data) are generating correctly. This indicates the issue may not be with data generation or display but specifically with the integration of the save functionality post-printing.

3. **Recommended Fix**:
   - Investigate the backend or frontend logic responsible for the post-printing save functionality:
     - Confirm that the callback for automatic saving is properly triggered after hitting "Print".
     - Verify if the invoice number auto-filling mechanism is intact, as it may be broken or ineffective when saving to designated folders.
     - Test the file-saving workflow, especially focusing on the folder path configuration, to ensure no errors are preventing the auto-save.
   - If a recent system update removed or disabled this automation, revert the change or reintroduce the feature.
   - Conduct end-to-end tests to validate that printing triggers saving actions in various scenarios.
