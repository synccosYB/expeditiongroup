#### Issue: Bank Reconciliation Discrepancy
1. **UI Affected Component**:
   - The "Bank Reconciliation" page.
   - Despite all transactions being marked as cleared, the page indicates a significant difference of `$2,791.61` between the "Cleared Balance" and the "Statement Balance."
   - The alert box states: **"The difference is $2,791.61. Continue marking transactions as cleared until the difference is $0.00."**
   - Both "Uncleared Transactions" and "Cleared Transactions" sections are displayed. Here, all transactions are cleared (Uncleared Transactions = 0).

2. **Root Cause Analysis**:
   - **Possibility A**: A recorded error in the `Statement Balance`, such as user input error or backend fetching the wrong statement data.
   - **Possibility B**: Transactions may not have synced properly or were omitted during reconciliation due to a mismatch in recorded dates or account associations.
   - **Possibility C**: The dollar difference could result from rounding issues or a bug in the calculation logic for transactions being applied to the Cleared and Statement Balances.

3. **Recommended Fixes/Next Steps**:
   - **Validation of Statement Balance**: Verify if the Statement Balance of `$185.28` is accurate and matches the actual bank statement.
   - **Backend Transaction Sync**: Check for any unprocessed or omitted transactions in the associated account's logs.
   - **Calculation Debugging**: Review system logs or backend logic to ensure calculations of balances and differences are being computed without errors (e.g., rounding issues).
   - **User Steps**: Instruct the user to revalidate all inputs and confirm the bank statement is correctly referenced in the system.
