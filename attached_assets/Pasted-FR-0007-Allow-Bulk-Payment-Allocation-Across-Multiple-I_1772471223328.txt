FR-0007
Allow Bulk Payment Allocation Across Multiple Invoices
Feature Request
New
Medium
Resolve
Status
New
Priority
Medium
Assigned To
Unassigned
Description

Add the ability to enter one lump-sum payment and allocate it across multiple open invoices, similar to QuickBooks Desktop.

Users should be able to:

Select multiple open invoices

Enter one total payment amount

Decide how much to apply toward each invoice

Automatically apply any remaining balance as a credit on the account

Currently, the system only allows payment toward one invoice at a time and does not support allocating a larger payment across multiple invoices.

AI Analysis

### Analysis of Bug Report: Bulk Payment Allocation Across Multiple Invoices

#### 1. **App Area Impacted**
   - The functionality for receiving and allocating payments in the "Undeposited Funds" section of the application.

#### 2. **Screenshot Observation**
   - The interface displays a "Payments Pending Deposit" section where invoices are listed individually.
   - Payments are currently applied to individual invoices as seen in the "Applied to INV..." details next to each payment.
   - There is no visible option for selecting multiple invoices or allocating a lump-sum payment across multiple invoices.
   - The "Receive Payment" button likely initiates a single payment allocation process.

#### 3. **Root Cause Analysis**
   - **Missing Functionality:**
     The system lacks a mechanism for allocating a single payment across multiple invoices. This could stem from:
     - A design limitation where the payment modal currently restricts allocation to one invoice.
     - Lack of support for credit balances or "remaining balance" auto-application.

#### 4. **Recommended Fix or Next Steps**
   - **Design Enhancement:**
     - Update the "Receive Payment" flow to enable the following:
       - Selection of multiple invoices during payment entry (e.g., a checkbox list in the modal).
       - A field for entering the total payment amount with invoice-specific allocation inputs (e.g., editable allocation amounts per invoice).
       - Automatic creation of an account credit for the remaining balance, if applicable.
   - **Back-End Update:**
     - Modify payment allocation logic to process and save allocations involving multiple invoices.
     - Implement logic for handling remaining balance as an account credit.
   - **UI/UX Testing:**
     - Ensure ease of use for bulk payment allocation and validate edge cases like partial allocation, zero remaining balance, and full payment.
   - **Regression Testing:**
     - Confirm that existing "single invoice" payment workflows are not negatively impacted.
